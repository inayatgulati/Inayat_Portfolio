About Me

UX/UI designer based in Vancouver — research-first, technically literate, and targeting full-time roles for fall 2026.

Based in Vancouver, BC

Designer who makes things actually work.

UX/UI Designer · Vancouver · SFU Interactive Arts & Technology

I design digital products that are as functional as they are considered. I got into UX because I kept encountering interfaces that looked the part but failed the person using them — and I wanted to be someone who closed that gap. My approach is research-first by habit and technically grounded by choice. A foundation in frontend development means I understand what actually ships — so I'm not designing beautiful mockups that fall apart the moment they hit a developer's hands. I care about the constraints as much as the craft.

I'm drawn to complex problems, cross-cultural nuance, and the details that most people skip over. Whether I'm synthesizing user research or pushing pixels, I'm always thinking about the person on the other side of the screen.

Outside of design, I'm usually exploring — Vancouver's neighbourhoods, BC's backcountry, or a city I've never been to before. Moving through different places and cultures keeps my perspective honest and my empathy for users sharp. I'm targeting full-time UX roles for fall 2026.

My Journey

2022 – 2023

Where Creativity Meets Evidence

Discovered that the best design decisions aren't opinions — they're hypotheses tested against real user behavior. Started building fluency in Figma, usability testing, and information architecture while studying at SFU. Realized design isn't about making things look good. It's about making things work.

Figma User Research Information Architecture Prototyping
2023 – 2024

Research Changes Everything

Moved from designing for imagined users to designing for real ones. Ran first structured usability tests, conducted contextual interviews, and learned to translate messy qualitative data into clear design decisions. The biggest lesson: the best solution is almost never your first idea.

Usability Testing Journey Mapping Persona Development Iterative Design
Summer 2025

Enterprise UX at Mastercard

Interned at Mastercard's Vancouver Tech Hub, redesigning internal SharePoint platforms for 15,000+ global employees. Worked directly under the VP of Workplace Experience, ran cross-cultural research across Dublin, India, and the US, and learned how to ship UX at enterprise scale — within real business constraints.

Enterprise UX Cross-cultural Research SharePoint Stakeholder Communication
Fall 2025

From Design to Product — End to End

Led the design and development of Chemtrails, a full-stack mobile travel app built in React Native with Firebase. Went from user interviews to shipped product — handling UX research, interaction design, and front/backend development. Proved that design thinking and technical execution aren't separate skills.

React Native Firebase Mobile UX End-to-end Product Design
